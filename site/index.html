Paladin

A control layer between intelligence and action.

Paladin is AFT's runtime-governance architecture for placing deterministic controls, human approval boundaries, state awareness, and evidence capture around AI-enabled systems. It is designed to help operators constrain what intelligent software can do before consequential actions reach real tools, data, hardware, or workflows.

01

Policy & action gates

Evaluate proposed actions against defined operating rules, risk conditions, permissions, and release boundaries.

02

Human authority

Keep accountable operators in control of consequential decisions and escalation paths.

03

System-state awareness

Track mission, runtime, telemetry, exceptions, and control conditions as part of the decision process.

04

Evidence & audit

Capture decisions, events, inputs, outputs, and review artifacts for engineering diligence and validation.

Aerospace & autonomous systems

Govern the mission layer, not just the model.

AFT is developing airframe-agnostic aerospace and UAS architectures where Paladin can govern mission state, operator authorization, payload workflows, telemetry, system health, exceptions, and AI-assisted functions around approved platforms.

The approach supports research and controlled evaluation for infrastructure inspection, emergency response, communications relay, mapping, logistics, medical-supply transport workflows, environmental monitoring, search support, test and evaluation, and defense-adjacent non-weaponized mission support.
